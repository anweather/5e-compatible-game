World squares, on paper

The world is a grid of valley-sized world squares, solved from a seed like a puzzle: every side must match its neighbour (rivers, ridges, canyons, coast), rivers only run downhill, and then roads are routed between the landmarks. Watch it solve, then walk: chunks near you go from fog to known to generated. Reroll for a new world, or turn on Torus and walk off an edge.
