World squares, on paper

The world is a grid of valley-sized world squares, solved from a seed like a puzzle: every side must match its neighbour (rivers, ridges, canyons, coast), rivers only run downhill, and then roads are routed between the landmarks. Watch it solve, then walk: chunks near you go from fog to known to generated. East and west wrap; north and south end in an ice wall and a sandstorm. Describe a world, find the best seeds for it, and keep the ones you like. Or draw the biomes first, in the World sketch below the map, and see the world squares fitted to your drawing.
