# Where things go

Spell Chemistry Valley, a 5E-compatible game built on SRD 5.2.1, takes ideas, bug reports and questions as GitHub
issues. Each kind has a form: **New issue** on the repository's Issues page, then pick one.

| You have | Use | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| An idea: a spell, a reaction, a place, a mini game, something it could do | **Idea** (labelled `enhancement`) | It waits until it is groomed: made into one or more tasks on the board, kept as an idea, or closed with the reason. Nothing is built straight from an idea. |
| Something that went wrong | **Bug report** (labelled `bug`) | It is reproduced from its evidence and becomes a task with a check that fails while the bug is there. |
| A question | **Question** (labelled `question`) | Answered in the issue; a question that turns out to be a decision goes to the decision log. |

Grooming adds an `area:` label and a priority (`p1` before the next release, `p2` soon, `p3` later), as
`docs/BACKLOG.md` says.
