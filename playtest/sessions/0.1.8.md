### The bot's and the runner's own gaps (not game bugs)

- **The runner crashes when the PC has no default audio output.**
  `AppMute.Mute` calls `GetDefaultAudioEndpoint`, which throws
  `COMException 0x80070490` (Element not found). Nothing catches it, so
  `playtest run` dies in its watch loop and leaves the player running: three
  orphaned players on the first try, killed by hand. For this session a local
  try/catch counted it as muted. That change was reverted and not committed. Fix:
  catch it in `PlaytestRun.Watch` (or have `AppMute.Mute` return 0 when there's
  no endpoint).
- **The bot can't reach the 12 combo spells.** Forge, Falling Star, Geyser,
  Wave, Frost, Rolling Boulder, Lightning (`lightning-bolt`), Rain Cloud, Sand
  Blast and Whirlwind are
  `radial: false` in `rules/spells.json`. `PlaytestPlayer.Options` offers only
  radial spells, and these are cast by elements (`rules/combos.json`). Wall of
  Stone and Plant Growth are on the radial and were cast. Reaching them needs a "cast by elements" goal in the bot and a rebuilt
  player, more than a list change. So this session didn't test casting by
  elements, the meter and cancel, or the brief's chains (Forge on mud, Geyser
  under a prop, Wave over a fire, a boulder through a window, stepping stones).
- **The throw goal never found an item in reach.** It failed 6 times ("ball 5,5#0
  is out of reach", "sending-stone 4,4#5 is out of reach"), wasting about 2
  minutes of runs.
- **The wizard got stuck at the lake shore** (811, frame 1172), on the way to
  the sea. It jumped and was "blocked" for 10 s, then the bot teleported. That
  was the bot's path, not the game.
