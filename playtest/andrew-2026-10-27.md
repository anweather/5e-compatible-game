# Andrew's playtest, 2026-10-27 (0.1.5 and dev)

Paper cuts from Andrew playing. Triaged by the coordinator, with the owning
lane in brackets. Not started: these wait for Thursday's planning (tokens).
P1 means small and very visible; P2 means polish and small features; P3
means bigger features.

## P1: bugs and clipping

1. **The defense-field sign** [Gameplay, DefenseFieldBuilder]:
   - the text clips through the sign;
   - it's mirrored on the sign's back;
   - it's wider than the sign.

   Fit the text (a bigger sign or shorter text). Audit every sign and label
   so that nothing shows MIRRORED TEXT on its back.
   - Idea: walk up and inspect a sign to get a dialogue box with the
     explanation, instead of a wall of text.
2. **Villagers' faces** look inverted or hollow ("kind of have faces, but not
   really there"; creepy) [Gameplay; Punchy humanoids]. Probably flipped
   normals or back-face culling on the face.
3. **Villagers sitting** [Gameplay, Folk and Seat]:
   - they sit 2–3 ft in front of the bench, facing the wrong way, so they
     bend backwards;
   - too many sit on one bench.

   One villager per seat slot.
4. **Village houses** [Gameplay, the village builders]:
   - roofs have no collision: let players jump onto rooftops;
   - door textures clip on some houses, mostly the block houses;
   - window shutters clip through the walls;
   - flower pots need collision;
   - small window details were mentioned, but unclear. Ask.
5. **The well**: you can walk through its wall; it needs collision. (Its water
   slowing you down is good; keep it.) [Gameplay]
6. **Hasten Time**: the sun moves in discrete steps, so all shadows jump.
   Smooth the sun and light between clock steps. [Gameplay, SkyLight]
7. **Under water**: there's no underwater effect, and looking up shows the
   normal sky. Add an underwater view: a tint, fog, a surface from below.
   [Gameplay]
8. **Deer on the beach**: wrong habitat; keep deer off sand and the coast.
   [Gameplay, ValleyFauna]
9. **Swimming** is too slow now that walking is faster. Raise it. [Gameplay]
10. **The ball court gate** only opens inward; it should open the way you're
    walking. [Gameplay, CourtGate]

## P2: polish and small features

11. **The HUD**: stack the vitals on the left, WoW-style: a placeholder
    character portrait, then health and magic below it, and stamina below
    those. The stamina bar could show only while it's in use (sprinting,
    climbing, swimming). [Gameplay]
12. **Water splashes**: jumping or walking in, and swimming. Plus mist or
    spray along the surf, and while swimming or walking in it. Should be
    cheap. [Gameplay view; it reads World's water]
13. **Ball court**: walls one log higher, so the ball stays in; fireworks or
    fanfare over the goal on a score. [Gameplay]
14. **The coast**: coconuts on the palm trees, and a pier out into the water
    off the island's south side (like the island's boat dock). [Gameplay
    builders]
15. **Spells**: Andrew couldn't find a "create light" spell. Is there one
    (Dancing Lights is an item; Daylight is a follow-up in J-020's notes)?
    Check and answer. [World and Gameplay]

## P3: bigger features (Thursday or later)

16. **Water reflections**: the water looks flat. Planar or screen-space
    reflections, or a reflection probe. Weigh the cost. [Build (URP) with
    Gameplay (the water shader)]
17. **A climbing animation** on vertical surfaces, like Breath of the Wild.
    Climbing itself already feels great. [Gameplay]
18. **Destructible, procedural rocks**: the big rock pieces could be pulled
    apart. Maybe generate the rocks procedurally instead of using models, so
    they can deform too. Fits the chemistry (hardness, earth shaping). [World
    with Gameplay; needs a design spike]
