- **Wall segment:** between two join points, on the grid, in a storey, with a height, a material (a terrain, so it burns or shatters like one) and a finish, and a list of openings.
- **Join points:** corner, T, cross, end. A rule for who trims whom, so walls connect with no code per shape.
- **Standard lengths:** in cells (1.5 m) and half cells, and standard openings: door, double door, windows, single and double garage doors, arch.
- **Floor and roof:** slab or plinth; a roof over a footprint of any outline: shape (gable, hip, shed, flat with parapet), pitch as rise over run, overhang, cover; details such as vigas, chimneys, porches as parts on a join or wall.
- **Two layers, one data format:** a building's parts rasterise to cells for the core, and the view draws the true geometry. Today a village building's footprint is all wall cells, a solid block, while a cell is 1.5 m, so real rooms don't fit inside walls a cell thick.
