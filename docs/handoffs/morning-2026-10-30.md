existing effects library:

  Pool), strike from above (Falling Star, replacing Flame Wave).
- Water: fill (Create Water), launch (Geyser, replacing Fog Bank), wave
  (Wave, replacing Flood), stick (Mud), freeze (Frost).
- Earth: dig (Raise Earth), transform (Forge, replacing Molten Boulder),

  replacing Dust Storm).
- Air: fan (Gust), conduct (Lightning jumps to metal and water, and powers
  machines), rain (Rain Cloud), scour (Sand Blast), carry (Whirlwind takes

  that spell.
