The ninth alpha of Spell Chemistry Valley, a 5E-compatible spell-chemistry sandbox. If you have 0.1.2 or later installed, it updates itself the next time you start it.

**Windows only again.** The Mac stays on 0.1.7 for now; a Mac on 0.1.7 and a PC on 0.1.9 can't play online together.

## Install (new players)

**Windows:** download `SpellChemistryValley-win-Setup.exe` and run it. The game isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn ("Unknown Publisher", or SmartScreen's *More info → Run anyway*).

**macOS:** this release has no Mac files; install [0.1.7](https://github.com/anweather/5e-compatible-game/releases/tag/alpha-0.1.7) for now, and it updates itself when the Mac catches up. From 0.1.7's page: download `SpellChemistryValley-osx-Portable.zip`, unzip it and move **Spell Chemistry Valley** into Applications (keep it out of Downloads and Documents). It isn't notarized, so the first launch is blocked: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click *Open Anyway*. The `.pkg` installer works too.

### Casting has weight
- The staff is gone: both hands are free for elements.
- Every cast is a move: one hand gathers the first element, the other the second, the hands slam together, press together while you charge, and thrust the spell out with a recoil.
- The slam and the release hit: a split-second freeze, a camera kick and controller rumble, scaled by the spell. An Esc-menu slider turns shake and rumble down or off.
- The casting has sounds: each element gathering, the slam, the charge rising and the release.

### Charged casts
- Hold the last element to charge a spell stronger, a level every half second. A tap still casts quickly.
- At full charge some spells change: Wall of Stone becomes a ramp, Raise Earth raises a tall pillar, and Frost becomes Sleet Storm, a wide slippery field.
- Charged Plant Growth brings burnt ground back to life.
- Charging slows you down, so a big cast is a commitment.

### Aiming and the screen
- New targeting markers show each spell's shape: a lane for waves, boulders and lightning, a ring that grows as you charge for bursts, an outline for what rises from the ground, a lock-on for a creature.
- A smaller HUD: stamina is a thin line under Health and Magic, shown only while you use it; smaller log text.
- The cast bar fills smoothly and shows the charge levels.
- Back closes the grimoire straight back to play.

### Spells and the world
- The wave and the rolling boulder go where you aim, at any angle; the wave carries props along and leaves water behind.
- Fire on a pool boils the water instead of baking brick under it.
- Snow falls and builds up, slows you down, and Frost glazes it into ice. Fog weather rolls in.
- Ice and frost keep your speed: you slide, and stopping and turning take time.
- Fire looks more natural: each flame sits and flickers a little differently.

### New things to do
- **Spell golf:** a ball on a tee on the north meadow. Hit it with Gust of Wind or pop it with Raise Earth, and sink it in as few strokes as you can.
- **A lightning puzzle:** east of the golf hole, a bolt into the pond powers a coil that opens a gate.
- **Swimming:** hold the dodge button to dive. Watch your breath: it runs out under water, and ice over your head is a trap.
- **A punch:** F on the keyboard, Circle on a pad. It cracks and breaks windows and knocks crates and balls about.

### Health and shapes
- At zero health you're knocked down, drop what you carry, and get up at the yard a few seconds later.
- Any cast button ends Shapeshift.

### The wizard
- No more glare on the wizard's head, and the hat and cowl fit the new head.
- The camera no longer zooms in when you walk up to a house.

### Performance
- Storms, snow and the forest at night are smoother, with fewer long hitches, and fog weather no longer stalls the game.

### Known issues
- In online games, the other player may miss the opening hand moves of your first cast. The spell itself is the same for everyone.

Bugs and the log: Esc → Open log folder, and send `Player.log`.
