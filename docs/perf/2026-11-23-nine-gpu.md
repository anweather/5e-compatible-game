# The nine's GPU frame on the Mac, and what grows with the map (2026-11-23)

The Mac (M2 Pro, 16 GB), a development player (`PrototypeBuild.MacOSProfiling`), windowed 1920 x 1080, v-sync and the
frame cap off. The nine ran under the 60 fps bar here (STATE "Next" item 1), GPU-bound: at the start (`-perf-scenario
stand -perf-at 4,23`) 29.6 ms mean, 33.5 p95, the GPU ~30 ms against a main thread of ~14. Andrew's question: the valley
ran fine on this Mac, so what grows with the world's size (J-086: cost follows the active region and the drawn tier,
never the map's size)?

## The answer first

- **Nothing grows from the nine to the Scale Island** (729 to 9,720 chunks) at the same spot: renderers, triangles, draws
  and memory are the same within a few percent. The rule holds past the nine.
- **From the valley to the nine, at the same cell and view, the frame grows 22.1 -> 29.5 ms** (GPU 22.6 -> 30.2), and
  almost all of it is one thing: **the brush cells' grass clumps on the generated land, drawn out to the haze (~730 m)**.
  11,388 of them were drawn at the start (7.4 M triangles, every one a shadow caster, nearly all past 300 m) against 42
  on the valley, whose camera stops at 500 m and whose own land has little brush. They cost 3.3 ms of GPU. Fix 1 gives
  them a LOD that ends them at 250 m.
- **The rest of the frame is the same on both maps and isn't about size.** The biggest costs are the terrains' detail
  grass (~7 ms), SSAO worked out before the opaques, which costs a depth prepass of everything (~5 ms; fix 2 works it out
  after them), and the sun's shadows (~4 ms).
- After both fixes the nine's start is **21.8 / 24.6 ms** (was 29.1 / 33.1, same build), the valley's 18.6 / 22.1 (was
  22.2 / 24.7). The 60 fps bar is not reached at the start: the grass stands in the way (below).

What grows, ranked by what it costs (the nine's GPU at the start, a part taken away with `PerfKnobs`):

1. **Brush clumps on the generated land, drawn to the haze: 3.3 ms**, 9.4 M triangles a frame. The valley draws its
   Default layer to 500 m; the nine to where its haze hides things (728 m), and the generated land is brush over wide
   stretches. The Resident Drawer ignores the layer's cut distance, and its small-mesh cull (0.4% of the screen) didn't
   take a clump at 300-700 m. Hiding the clumps past 150 m or past 300 m saved the same 3.3 ms: 11,346 of the 11,388 were
   past 300 m, a few pixels each in a haze of 60-95%.
2. **Tree impostors to the haze: part of 2.0 ms** (the whole impostor cost on the nine; 4,525 drawn against the valley's
   1,411, which stop at 500 m). Not changed.
3. **Water tiles, far land, terrains, lights: none measurable** (hiding the water tiles or the far land changed nothing;
   more lights on the island are lights of villages out of view, culled by Forward+).

## Fix 1: brush clumps end at 250 m

`CellDressing.DrawBrushNear`: a brush cell's root gets a `LODGroup` with one LOD (its clumps) that ends where a 2 m cell
is as high on screen as it is at 250 m (`BrushDrawMetres`) with the main camera's field of view and the quality's LOD
bias. A LOD, not a layer cut: the Resident Drawer culls LOD groups on the GPU but ignores the layer's cut distance, and the
Spyglass's zoom draws them further, as it should. The terrain's own grass ends at 120 m.

| The nine's start, same build | Mean | Median (fps) | p95 | GPU mean | Triangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brush to the haze (`-perf-brush-far`, with SSAO before) | 29.13 | 29.26 (34) | 33.10 | 29.9 | 22.6 M |
| Brush to 250 m (SSAO before) | 25.98 | 25.99 (38) | 30.30 | 26.7 | 13.4 M |

The look: stills of the same view with time stopped, with the LOD and without (`-perf-look-pair`, from the wizard and from
60 m up looking each way): 0.0-1.4% of pixels differ by more than 8 of 255 from up high, and the ground view's 2.4% is
SSAO's (fix 2). The clumps that go are past 250 m, mostly behind the forest at this start. Stills in
`/Users/anweather/5e-logs/fps-fix-1123/shots/` (`look4-*-before-top-now-bottom.jpg`).

The check: `BrushLodTests` (the LOD ends at `BrushDrawMetres` and that stays between the grass's 120 m and 300 m), and
the census line (`Shader_Graphs/TerrainGrass` drawn) in any `-perf-scenario` run.

## Tools added

- `PerfCensus`: `PERF CENSUS start|end` lines in every `-perf-scenario` run, outside the measured frames: renderers by
  group (scene root / its child) and by shader, enabled / drawn last frame / shadow casters / triangles drawn; terrains,
  tree impostors, cameras, lights, URP's settings.
- `PerfKnobs`: player flags that take one part away as measuring starts (`-perf-shadows-off`, `-perf-hard-shadows`,
  `-perf-cascades N`, `-perf-post-off`, `-perf-ssao-off`, `-perf-ssao-after-opaque`, `-perf-ssao-before-opaque`,
  `-perf-fog-off`, `-perf-render-scale S`, `-perf-grass-off`, `-perf-detail-metres M`, `-perf-detail-density D` (did
  nothing: a terrain's density set at runtime didn't change what it drew), `-perf-grass-no-shadows`, `-perf-terrain-off`,
  `-perf-impostors-off`, `-perf-brush-far`, `-perf-gpu-occlusion`, `-perf-small-mesh P`, `-perf-hide A,B`,
  `-perf-hide-shader S [-perf-hide-beyond M]`).
- `-perf-look-pair`: at a run's end, time stopped, stills of the view now and as it drew before these fixes.
