# Performance pass, macOS (steps 1 and 2: 2026-10-31)

The numbers from step 1 of `docs/handoffs/perf-pass-brief.md`, and their causes
from step 2 ("Attribution (step 2)", below). The comparison and issues are step
3. How it ran, for the future `perf-pass` skill: `notes-2026-10-31-mac.md`.

## In short

- **#5, the forest-at-night hitch, is reproduced on the Mac and explained.** The
  shipped pipeline-state collection has no night states, so Metal compiles them
  mid-walk: 442 and 226 ms when `SpeedTree8_PBRLit` and Lit first draw at night.
  Warming up from a night trace removes both (worst frame 41.5 ms). Chunk
  borders cost nothing measurable, and walking spawns no things.
- **Fog weather stops the game.** About 285 ms a frame as it arrives, then single
  frames of 52 to 68 s, from making 3 GameObjects for each fog cell (0.33 ms a
  puff). The rules really do ask for 190 000 fog cells: about 77% of open cells.
- **Storm:** 52 ms p50, frames of 500 to 600 ms. One film over the whole valley
  is redrawn for a few cells of mud (190 cells, a box of 105 165). The world runs
  at under half speed because 15 frames a second of 6 ms budget can't pay for a
  38 ms tick. That follows from the slow frames.
- **Snow:** a frame every 4 s or so, growing from 450 to 910 ms over the 30 s:
  each snowfall recomputes the union of its cells' rectangles, chunk by chunk.
- **Bot route:** p95 about 190 ms, 2500 frames over 50 ms. The river's level
  changes on 86% of ticks and the bot draws up to 24 water tiles a frame at
  14 to 20 ms a tile. With a real budget the same work is 42 ms on every frame.
- **Fire and aiming are cheap:** flames add at most 4 ms of GPU even as a wall,
  and the aim marker takes 0.04 ms a frame. A close wall of fire is still 81 ms
  of GPU: 57 ms of that is lit smoke.
- **Meadow, wildfire, village:** fine, 12 to 17 ms mean with nothing over 50 ms.
- **Flames draw on Metal, and so does Particles/Lit.** The 6 pipeline errors
  come from warm-up only; no real draw raised one.

## Method

`-fps-probe -perf-scenario <name>` (`PerfScenario.cs`, `PerfStage.cs`,
`PerfMarkers.cs`). Each scenario runs 3 times, in rounds rather than in a block.
Frame times are real time between `Update`s, with the cell counts' own time
taken out.

| Scenario | Set-up | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| meadow | Noon, clear, the `perf` probe's camera | 30 s after 5 s settling |
| wildfire | Noon, clear, Wildfire mod, 5 Fire Bolts, the `wildfire` probe's camera | 20 s from the last bolt |
| storm | Noon, weather held to storm by a rule switch, meadow camera | 30 s after 5 s |
| snow | Noon, weather held to snow, default rates, meadow camera | 30 s after 5 s |
| fog | Noon, weather held to fog, meadow camera | 60 s from the start, unsettled |
| village | Noon, clear, the wizard in Hollowmere's square, fixed camera | 30 s after 5 s |
| bot | `-playtest -playtest-seed 811 -playtest-seconds 480` | From 8 s in to the bot's end, about 650 s of real time |

The bot's game time stays locked at 1/30 s a frame with its frame cap off, so a
frame takes only the time it needs and a run takes as long as its frames do. Its
175 or so frames round its own screenshots are left out (worst 257 to 275 ms).

### 1. #5, the walking hitch, and the 858 ms frame

**Culprit, proven on Metal: `Resources/PsoMetal.graphicsstate` has no night
states, so the first draw of each stalls the frame while Metal compiles it.**
`forest` walks the wizard by the stick at 21:30 through F-0002's chunks and on
in a ring (6,7 > 6,6 > 5,6 > 5,5 > 4,5 ...), 120 s, clear.

| Run | Metal cache | Warm-up | Worst frames (ms) | Stalled frame after the first teleport (ms, before measuring) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| forest-1 | new to this route | shipped, 253 states | 445.6, 225.9 | 2834 |
| forest-cold | emptied | shipped | 441.8, 226.1 | 3907 |
| forest-wide, trace-2, warm2 | warm | shipped | 53.9, 60.7, 43.1 | none over 100 |
| forest-fixed | emptied | **a night trace of this route, 157 states** | **41.5**, 40.2 | none over 100 |

- **Where the time goes:** `PostLateUpdate.FinishFrameRendering` 430 ms,
  `Inl_RenderCameraStack` 425 ms, `Semaphore.WaitForSignal` 427 ms. Script,
  chemistry, physics and terrain markers were all under 10 ms in that frame.
  It reads as a wait because the compile is on another thread (likely: `sample` in a
warm run shows `CreatePSOCacheEntry` off the main thread).
- **Which states:** with `-pso-trace`, the frames of the two hitches create
  `Universal Render Pipeline/Nature/SpeedTree8_PBRLit` passes 0 and 3 and one Lit
  state (442 ms), then Lit and SpeedTree8 pass 1 (226 ms), all with
  `_MAIN_LIGHT_SHADOWS LOD_FADE_CROSSFADE`. That's the forest's trees.
- **Why night:** the route's night trace has 61 of its 157 states missing from
  PsoMetal, 46 of the variant lines with `_CASTING_PUNCTUAL_LIGHT_SHADOW`. The same
  route at noon has 13 of 109 missing. RELEASING.md's traces are bot runs, which
  stay in daylight (`-perf-pso-diff` lists them).
- **The first Metal cache only hides it:** once compiled, macOS keeps the program,
  so the hitch shows once per install or update (likely) and a second run misses
  it. That would explain why F-0002's replay of run 3 didn't show it.
- **Cleared, proven:** chunk switching and thing spawning. On the walk, 17 chunks
  on and 10 off: `ChemGrid.ChunkPlan` peak 0.4 ms, `ChunkApply` and `ChunkEvent`
  0.0 ms, `ValleyGround.DrawDetails` 0.0 ms over 34 changes,
  `Terrain.Details.Render` peak 1.5 ms. `ThingSpawner.SpawnChunk` isn't called at
  all: every thing spawns at Start. No chunk had catch-up ticks to run here, so
  a chunk that left live and came back isn't covered.
- **On the PC (likely):** the same mechanism fits the RC's 185 ms: night, trees,
  nothing cast. `PsoDirect3D12` wasn't inspected and D3D12 wasn't run.
- **858 ms:** no record of it exists, in the repo or on any branch, beyond the brief
  and this file. The nearest is `playtest/sessions/0.1.8.md`: `frame_ms_worst` of
  707 to 725 ms in the 3 RC runs, guessed to be the first frame. That guess holds
  here (item 8), so 858 ms is likely the same metric on another run. Likely only.
- **A steady cost, not a hitch:** the forest runs at 25 ms p50 with 26 ms of GPU
  against the meadow's 11.5 ms. Night and day match (25.3 and 25.4 ms p50).

**Fix, under 30 minutes on each machine:** the RELEASING.md recipe with
`forest` as well as the bot: `-fps-probe -perf-scenario forest -perf-seconds 120
-pso-trace <file>`, merged with `PsoCollections.Merge`. The shipped 253 states
warm up in 73 ms on a warm cache and 11.5 s on an empty one, so a first launch
already waits about 11 s; more states add to both.

### 2. Water

| Run | `ChemGrid.WaterMesh` mean/peak | `Water.Recompute` | `Water.DrawTile` | Tiles drawn | ms a tile | Verts a tile |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| storm | 27.6 / 55.6 | 12.9 (283 of 448 frames) | 14.6 | 480 | 13.6 | 870 |
| storm `-perf-exact` | 366.8 / 439.8 | 20.0 | 346.7 | 1464 (24 a frame) | 14.4 | not read |
| bot | 22.6 / 280.5 | 2.7 | 19.9 | 13 634 | 20.7 | 2830 |
| bot `-perf-time-budget` | 24.4 / 66.9 | 3.5 | 20.9 | 11 921 | 25 | 3550 |

- **Two costs, proven.** `Recompute` walks every wet cell in the world (5900 to
  9300) whenever any changed: 21 ms a time in storm, for at most 378 changed cells.
  `ComputeTops` is 84% of it. Then `DrawTile` redraws a 32 x 32 tile.
- **In a tile, proven:** `Contour.Tile.Outline` 10.3 ms of 14.6 in storm, 8.4 of
  20 in the bot; `Points` 4.2 and 11.3; upload 0.1. So it isn't the mesh upload.
- **Inside Outline, likely:** every lattice point gets two octaves of noise, wet
  or not, over 17 689 points (`CellContour.Sample`), and a tile builds water and ice
  even with no ice. Inside Points, `FlowAndFoam` and `BlendAround` run per vertex.
- **The budget doesn't hold, proven:** one tile takes 14 to 25 ms, and the 3 ms
  budget is only checked between tiles. In storm the backlog stands at 184 dirty
  tiles on average, peak 219, at 1 drawn a frame, so it never clears.
- **The bot's frames over 50 ms, proven:** rebuilds on 2436 frames, matching the
  2489 spikes, in nearly clear weather (198 of the top 200 frames). The river's
  water level changes on 86% of ticks (2436 of 2835): 78% of changed cells are on
  the river, 81% are level changes with the cell staying wet. Worst chunks: 5,4,
  which holds the river. It isn't rain. Each drew 5.6 tiles, up to 14 in a frame.
- **`ExactDrawTiles = 24` is the bot's own spike, proven:** with a real budget the
  same route is p50 42 ms (was 19), p95 78 ms (was 189), worst 223 ms (was 432),
  mean 42.9 ms (was 45.3). The work is the same. A player by the river pays
  about 20 ms on every frame, and the bot's picture of spikes is a batching effect.
- **Garbage:** a slick rebuild is 16.7 MB (17 frames, storm), a rebuild with a
  Recompute 4 MB, and 18 MB for 8 river tiles in the bot's worst frames (about
  2.2 MB a river tile). So step 1's 20 MB "water" is several tiles or a slick.
  From the code (likely): `CellContour` allocates 4 arrays each Build, 150 KB for a
  tile surface and about 14 MB for the film; the rest is closures per vertex and
  the `DryAround` and `WetNeighbours` iterators.

**`ChemGrid.SlickMesh`, proven:** a whole rebuild for a small change. Storm: 17
rebuilds in 30 s at 536 ms peak, each for 1 to 27 cells of mud turning, 190 film
cells at most, drawn as one mesh over a box of 105 165 cells: 553 times the film.
`Contour.Film.Outline` is 515 of the 536 ms; 7733 verts come out of about 1.7 M
lattice points. Bot: 41 rebuilds, 165 ms peak, box 31 964, 30 film cells.

### 3. Storm's world at under half speed

Proven. A storm tick costs **37.8 ms** (meadow 18.7 ms), and every one of the 448
frames ran out of budget, doing 83 ticks. At 6 ms a frame and 15 frames a second
the grid gets about 100 ms a second; 6 ticks cost 227 ms. At 40 fps it would

### 5. Fog

- **`FogBank.LateUpdate` is the LateBehaviourUpdate, proven:** 179.0 of 180.1 ms
  mean, 358 of 359 ms peak: **1.02 us a puff** at 117 150 squares.
- **`RenderGas`, proven:** 1784.6 ms mean, of which `FogBank.MakePuffs` is 1782.9.
  That's creation: 117 150 squares made in 2 frames, 0.99 ms a square, 0.33 ms a puff.
- **Reuse, proven:** fog reuses, but rarely: 4778 squares reused against 117 150
  made, and 5016 freed. A square is only freed when its gas has faded.
- **3 GameObjects a cell, yes:** `PuffsPerSquare = 3` on every fog cell (fog isn't
  thinned by `GasDrawnAt`), so 351 000 objects, about 3 KB each: 1 GB (allocated
  1529 against 494 MB).
- **190 000 is what the rules ask for (likely, by arithmetic):** `Veil` gives open
  cells a unit at 0.05 a tick and fog decays at 0.015 a tick, so about 77% of open
  cells hold fog, which is about 205 000 of 266 000 in 65 chunks. The glossary says
  "here and there" and "too thin to hide anything", so the count may not be what
  was meant. That's a question for Andrew: the rule or the drawing.
