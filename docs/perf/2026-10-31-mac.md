# Performance pass, macOS (step 1: measured 2026-10-31)

The numbers from step 1 of `docs/handoffs/perf-pass-brief.md`. Attribution in
depth is step 2 and the comparison and issues are step 3; neither is here yet.
How it ran, for the future `perf-pass` skill: `notes-2026-10-31-mac.md`.

## In short

- **Fog weather stops the game.** About 285 ms a frame as it arrives, then single
  frames of 52 to 68 s. In a 300 s run they fell to 23, 11 and 5 s, and it settled
  at about 1000 ms a frame with 190 000 fog cells and 27 ticks done. `FogBank` makes
  3 GameObjects for every cell of fog.
- **Storm:** 52 ms p50, frames of 500 to 600 ms (`ChemGrid.SlickMesh`), and the
  world runs at under half speed (about 83 ticks in 30 s, not 180).
- **Snow:** a frame every 4 s or so, growing from 450 to 910 ms over the 30 s
  (`ChemGrid.Shaping`).
- **Bot route:** p95 about 190 ms, 2500 frames over 50 ms (`ChemGrid.WaterMesh`,
  170 to 280 ms and about 20 MB of garbage a rebuild).
- **Meadow, wildfire, village:** fine, 12 to 17 ms mean with nothing over 50 ms.
- **Flames draw on Metal** (below).
- **Not reproduced:** #5, the forest-at-night hitch, and the RC's 858 ms frame.
  This route stayed in daylight.

**A development build isn't a release build.** Script and logging overhead are
higher, and no non-development player was measured, so these are upper figures.
The HUD was on in every scenario.

## Method

`-fps-probe -perf-scenario <name>` (`PerfScenario.cs`, `PerfStage.cs`,
`PerfMarkers.cs`). Each scenario runs 3 times, in rounds rather than in a block.
Frame times are real time between `Update`s, with the cell counts' own time
taken out.

| Scenario | Set-up | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| meadow | Noon, clear, the `perf` probe's camera | 30 s after 5 s settling |
| wildfire | Noon, clear, Wildfire mod, 5 Fire Bolts, the `wildfire` probe's camera | 20 s from the last bolt |
| storm | Noon, weather held to storm by a rule switch, meadow camera | 30 s after 5 s |
| snow | Noon, weather held to snow, default rates, meadow camera | 30 s after 5 s |
| fog | Noon, weather held to fog, meadow camera | 60 s from the start, unsettled |
| village | Noon, clear, the wizard in Hollowmere's square, fixed camera | 30 s after 5 s |
| bot | `-playtest -playtest-seed 811 -playtest-seconds 480` | From 8 s in to the bot's end, about 650 s of real time |

The bot's game time stays locked at 1/30 s a frame with its frame cap off, so a
frame takes only the time it needs and a run takes as long as its frames do. Its
175 or so frames round its own screenshots are left out (worst 257 to 275 ms).

## The fire on Metal

**Prototype/Flame draws on Metal.** Written check on the fb8540a player, wildfire
scenario, one run, 1 440 000 pixels:

| View | Flame-coloured pixels, flames on (2 shots) | Flames off | Changed by the flames (over 30 of 765) | Changed by flicker alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Close, 5 m from a burning cell | 226 704 and 189 792 | 268 | 722 735 | 554 777 |
| The wildfire camera, at the end | 2 390 and 2 389 | 2 382 | 4 680 | 2 962 |

- `PERF FLAMES cells=482 shader_supported=True`.
- No log has an error naming Flame, a compute error or an exception.
- **The logs aren't error-free:** every run logs the same 6 lines, `Metal: Error
  creating pipeline state (Universal Render Pipeline/Particles/Lit): Fragment
  input(s) TEXCOORD2, TEXCOORD3 mismatching vertex shader output`. That's URP's
  own particle shader, at start-up. What it stops drawing wasn't checked.

**The wildfire's GPU number is low for a fire.** The flames-off shot from the
wildfire camera barely differs: by the end of the 20 s the camera sees little
flame (a 480 px shot confirmed it, the only image used). GPU time there is 9 ms
against the meadow's 11.5 ms, so this scenario doesn't measure what flames cost
the GPU. Its CPU numbers stand.

## Other numbers

- **Burning cells:** peak 594 at the 2 s samples, against the 650 asked for.
- **Fog cells:** peak 116 883 in two 60 s runs and 59 377 in the third, and 193 636
  in the 300 s run, of about 266 000 cells in 65 active chunks. Not every active
  cell yet, but it was still climbing while only 27 ticks ran in 300 s. Whether
  it would reach them all wasn't followed further.
- **Plain `-fps-probe`, for the 2026-10-25 Mac check** (one run): showcase 29.3 ms
  mean, 49.0 ms p95, 181 ms worst; fire 18.1 ms mean, 26.8 ms p95, 111 ms worst.
  Not comparable with 16.3 ms then: that was a release build of an older game.
- **Play-mode entry:** about 4 s, against about 60 s last time. `tools/probe.sh
  perf --seconds 1` took 6.2 to 6.4 s of wall time in 4 runs, about 2 s of which is
  the probe's settle and hold. That's an estimate, and it includes leaving Play
  mode. Domain reload is off in Editor Settings.

## What couldn't be measured

- **#5, the forest at night, and the RC's 858 ms frame.** Seed 811 on this build
  didn't take the RC's route: the bot picks goals against `playtest/coverage.json`,
  which isn't on the Mac, and the RC's `run.json` stayed on the PC. The clock ran
  from about 9:00 to 17:00, so there was no night. The worst measured frame was
  432 ms. The bot's own report has 961 ms in bot-1, which isn't in the table and
  wasn't attributed: it's either before measuring began or a screenshot frame.
- **The same route three times.** Frame counts differ a little (14 003 to 14 265
  measured) and the goals may too; replays are only promised on the same build.
- **A wall of flames on the GPU**, as above.
- **Windows.** The PC repeats this.
