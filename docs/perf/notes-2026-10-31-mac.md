## What went wrong, and what to do about it

- **Bot runs need a limit of 1500 s.** I gave 700 s, for 480 s of game time at
  better than real speed. It's slower than real time here (about 47 ms a frame
  against 33 ms of game time), so the first two reached frames 14 280 and 14 430
  of 14 400 and were ended with no result line, because the bot writes it only as
  it quits. A third was stopped on purpose. All 3 were redone. Those are in `killed/`.
- **A run that ends inside a slow frame loses that frame.** A fog run's first
  result had 16 frames, worst 296 ms, over 63 s: the one-minute frame was missing,
  because the coroutine finished before the next `Update` could record it. `Update`
  now ends the run. Rule: check that frames × mean is close to seconds.
- **Fog looked like a hang.** `sample <pid> 3 -file f.txt` on the player found
  the cause in a minute even with no symbols for scripts, and is worth doing on
  anything stuck: the native frames named `CreateGameObject` and PhysX.
- **The marker list comes first.** My first guesses had 3 names that don't exist
  (`Physics.Processing`, `Render Thread`, `Terrain.Heightmap.RenderPatches`). The
  `missing=` field on `PERF MARKERS` says which. A recorder finds a marker by name,
  whatever category it's given. `Camera.Render` exists and reads 0 under URP.
- **No markers exist for chunk activation** or thing spawning, which step 2 needs
  for #5. `PlaytestBot` had none despite the brief; it has `PlaytestBot.LateUpdate`
  and `PlaytestBot.Shot` now.
- **`EditorApplication.EnterPlaymode()` from `script eval` did nothing** in an
  unfocused Editor, and my poll loop waited 10 minutes for it. Use the probe.
- **A flames-off shot from far away proves nothing:** the fire had moved out of
  shot. The close view with a flicker baseline is the real check.
- **`python3` here has Pillow but no numpy.** Plain loops over 1.4 M pixels take
  seconds. `==` in a zsh `echo` is an error; write `--`.
- **Short fog runs mislead.** 60 s showed one long frame and hid the rest, and
  which order they came in: the 300 s run (`fog-long.log`, limit 620 s) showed the
  whole shape. Read frame numbers and `t=` before saying what comes after what.
- **Seed 811 isn't the RC's route** without `playtest/coverage.json` and the RC's
  `run.json`. To check #5 on a Mac, copy those over or start a run at night.
