# Performance pass, Windows (2026-10-31)

Step 1 of `docs/handoffs/perf-pass-brief.md`, repeated on the PC: the same seven
scenarios as `2026-10-31-mac.md`, in the same table shape. Measurement only.
Attribution stays with the Mac; markers here are only read to name what's over budget.

## In short

The PC runs its ordinary scenes at 9 to 12 ms in a release player, and nothing
freezes it. Every number is drawn at 1080p into a texture, with nothing presented to a
window, because the Windows session is Disconnected.

frame over 100 ms). Everything over budget is already on the Mac's list.

- **The perf harness was broken on dev, and every scenario with it.** 8c3d9c6 added
  `TickStage.Dragon`, `PerfCounts` wasn't told, and with `-perf-scenario` every tick's
  end threw in `ChemGrid.Update`: 4560 exceptions in 8 s, 0 ticks, 3.5 ms frames, hour
  stuck at 9. A one-line fix is on local branch `perf-tick-stage-dragon` (add069e), not
  on dev. **Until it's merged, any `-perf-scenario` run on dev is false, on the Mac too.**
  Players without the flag aren't touched.
- **The split in 1e8d899 broke nothing.** All seven scenarios ran with the fix above.
- **Storm is the one scene that misses:** 25 fps, 215 ms frames, the world at 92% speed.
- **Fog in the player is +1.3 ms over the meadow,** not the Editor's +8.2 ms, but this
  fog isn't the Mac's: 19 100 fog cells after #28's patches against 220 668, and none
  drawn (#34). Drawing it is still to pay for.
- **New bug, seen by chance:** in 1 of 4 full bot runs the robe's cloth went to NaN at
  frame 407 and stayed so to the end, 6421 errors, and the wizard lost the skirt. See
  "Robe".
- **Release against development:** 12 to 16% on most scenes, 23% in storm, 35% in the
  bot. So the Mac's figures, all development, are upper figures by about that much
  (likely; no Mac release was measured).

## Method

Same flags and same times as the Mac: `-fps-probe -perf-scenario <name> -perf-offscreen`,
30 s (wildfire 20 s, fog 60 s with `-perf-seconds 60`), the bot `-playtest -playtest-seed
811 -playtest-seconds 480`. One player at a time, in rounds: release, development,
release, then a third bot and a second development round. The third bot run is there
because bot-1 and bot-2 differ by 13% in frames over 50 ms and worst frame; the mean
differs by 8%. Everything else repeats within 3%.

**Did it draw?** The agreed test, `cpu_render` nonzero, can't pass here: FrameTimingManager
reports nothing without a present (`cpu_render` 0.0 in release, 0.1 in development; GPU
0.0). The bot's GPU column reads 13 ms, from the frames round its own shots, so it isn't
used. So each run is checked another way, and all of them passed:

| Check | No offscreen (control, meadow, 8 s) | `-perf-offscreen` (meadow) |
|---|---|---|
| Frame mean | 7.8 ms | 10.2 ms |
| GPU busy, `nvidia-smi` | 7% | 49% |
| Video memory in use, whole GPU | 1838 MB | 1940 MB |
| `gfx_used_mb` (development) | not run | 442 MB, against the Mac's 338 at 1600x900 |

Every measured run shows the GPU 29 to 53% busy (sampled every 5 s, the last three
quarters of a run), ticks at the expected rate and no 4 ms frames. Nothing was thrown
out for not drawing. **No pixel of that texture was looked at**; the bot's JPEGs are
drawn by its own camera call. The one set of numbers thrown out is the pre-fix build's.

Release doesn't read markers, Gfx or the frame's garbage, so those are "n/a" below, and
it can't tell the bot's own shots from its frames: the release bot rows include them,
the development row leaves out 145 frames (worst 112.9 ms). `System` still reads in
release.

## Robe

In bot-3 (release, frame 407, chunk 4,4, goal "go to the waterfall") the log starts
`Mesh 'Wizard robe (cloth)': abnormal mesh bounds` with NaN bounds, 6421 times to the end
of the run. The bot's shots show the skirt gone: `007281-goal.jpg` there against
`007429-goal.jpg` in bot-1, on the same stretch of river, robe on (the shots weren't kept). Bot-1, bot-2 and the development bot have
none. Its numbers are kept: the run's mean, p95 and worst sit inside the others'.

The bot's log hook then took a shot from inside that culling call, so URP logged
`Recursive rendering is not supported` and `UniversalCameraData has already been
created`, and the bot recorded a black picture at frame 407. That one is the bot's doing.

## What couldn't be measured

- **GPU time on this PC.** FrameTimingManager gives nothing without a present, so
  `GPU %` is the only figure, and #31 (GPU climbing late in a storm) can't be looked at
  from here.
- **A presented frame.** A session with a desktop would give that, and the HUD's real
  cost.
- **Pixels in the offscreen texture.** See Method.
- **Temperature and the power plan** weren't checked.
- **The same route each time.** Goals 43 to 44, frames 13 869 to 14 124 measured.
- **Why storm's development p95 is so far above release's.**
