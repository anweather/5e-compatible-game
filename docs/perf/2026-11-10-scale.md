# What doesn't scale: the nine against the scale island

The PC, 2026-11-10, on `scale-12x10` (u2's map, e6c1d8ab, merged with dev eb48f0f5; this file and the probe flags at
the commit that adds them). The brief is `docs/handoffs/briefs/scale-probe.md`; the rule it tests is J-086: cost
follows the active region and the drawn tier, never the map's size. The nine is 1728 x 1728 cells, 729 chunks; the
scale island (`world/maps/scale-12x10/`) is 6912 x 5760 cells, 9,720 chunks (13.3 times as many), with the nine pinned
whole in its middle.

## The headline

1. **The full bake fails** (the first finding). Making all 9,720 chunks works and is fast on the PC (19 s on 16
   threads), but the last step throws: `places.json: place dunmere is listed twice`. Two lake villages (and two great
   trees) drew the same name from their template's short lists (`villageNames` / `landmarkNames` in
   `world/landmarks/village-with-landmark.json`, six to eight names each), so their places share an id
   (`the-elder-oak`, `dunmere`, `fogmere`), and `WorldPlaces.Generate` refuses the file it reads back. The nine and
   u2's 6 x 6 copy have one of each, which is why `dotnet test core` passed. A player's first launch on the map would
   throw the same way (`ChemGrid.Baked` -> `GeneratedMap.EnsureBakedInto`). For the probe only, the scratch harness
   renamed the repeats (`dunmere-2`, ...) and finished the bake's last two files as `Bake` writes them; nothing in the
   branch changes for it.
2. **The scale island doesn't load in a player on this PC.** With the bake in place, a development player made the
   9,720 chunk `Terrain`s in 35-38 s (the nine: 0.4 s), then free RAM reached 0 before the world was built, and the
   run was stopped by its pid both times (once at 0.45 GB free, once at 0.25 GB). The PC had 2-4.4 GB free (the
   Editor holds 11 GB private after a full EditMode run, u2's VM 5.6 GB). The nine's player already commits 2.9 GB at
   load and 3.3-3.9 GB in play; the World alone adds 3.4 GB at the scale island's size (below). So none of the
   scenarios ran on the scale island: the frame tables are the nine's, and the list says what each cost becomes at
   9,720 chunks, from the code and from what the load did reach.

- Players: `SpellChemistryValley.exe -logFile <log> -screen-fullscreen 0 -screen-width 1920 -screen-height 1080
  -fps-probe -perf-offscreen -perf-memory -perf-scenario <...> [-map nine | -map scale-12x10]`, one at a time, each
  sampled once a second from outside and stopped by its own pid if free RAM fell below the floor.
- New on this branch: every run logs `PERF LOAD` (the map, seconds since start-up, chunks, active chunks) as
  `PerfScenario` starts; `-perf-memory` adds `PERF MEMORY` lines at the start, as measuring begins and at the end
  (`PerfMemory`: psapi private / working set / peak on Windows, Unity's native, managed and driver totals, every
  object counted and sized by type); `-perf-scenario walkout` walks east from the nine's spawn, four chunks inside
  the nine's east edge, sixteen chunks (onto the grass on the island, to the edge on the nine); `-perf-switches`
  makes a sim-only switch and a look switch during a fixed-camera run (`PerfStage.Scale.cs`).
- The nine's bot walk ran 300 s of game time (10,128 frames at 1/30 s) in 144 s.

## Terrains for the drawn tier (2026-11-10, PC, branch `terrains-drawn-205`, #205)

The list's 3 and 4. A generated map makes no chunk terrain at Awake now. Once its first world binds, `MapTerrains`
makes terrains for the chunks within the far ring's drawn dial plus one (sight 4 + 1, Chebyshev) of each character and
the camera, and for those under the active chunks, nearest first; in play about 4 ms a frame, with the next chunks'
arrays made on a worker eight at a time. A chunk past the dial plus two goes back to a pool, and the pool's terrains are
used again (the walk east made none after the first 146). A chunk's arrays (`GroundFromLand.Lazy`, `PinnedGround.LayLazily`:
the same arrays as made whole, `ChunksMadeOneAtATimeAreTheSameAsMadeWhole`) and its TerrainData are made with its
terrain; an untouched chunk gives its data back when its terrain goes (to be reused), a changed one keeps it. A cell of a
chunk not made stands on the map's land (what the drawn ground matches to a land unit), and paint, grass and burnt trees
waiting for a chunk are put on when it's made. Grounding outside the made chunks (`TerrainTiles`, `RowHouses.TerrainY`,
the sea's depth) asks `MapTerrains.HeightAt`, which falls back to the ground. The valley keeps its 81, made whole.

## Start-up by chunk (2026-11-10, PC, branch `startup-by-chunk`, #206, #207)

The list's 5 and 7, the view's half. J-086: view cost follows the active region and the drawn tier, never the map's
size. What's per chunk now:

- **The cells' views** (`ChemGrid.ViewBlocks`): a block a chunk, made as the chunk first draws, not a reference a cell of
  the map (24 MB on the nine, 320 MB on the island).
- **The cells' effects texture** (`CellFxTexture`): a window of at most 1024 x 1024 cells that repeats, a cell's texel
  its x and y modulo the window, so the water's shader is unchanged; a chunk takes its slot when it first shows an effect.
  The valley fits and keeps a texel a cell. 12 MB (and the GPU's copy) on the nine and 160 MB on the island become 4 MB;
  the flashes' brightness is kept only for texels flashing.
- **The heightfield** (`CellHeightfield.ResetLazily`): no chunk worked out at the bind; a chunk is when first asked for
  (its terrain made, a height read, an update reaching it), the drawn tier's side by side (`Prepare`, also the next eight
  in play), and one whose terrain goes back to the pool untouched lets its offsets go. The same bits as the whole Reset
  (`HeightfieldThreadingTests`). The valley still resets whole.
- **The ground's base heights and paint** (`CellsByChunk`): a block a chunk terrain, made with the chunk; an unmade
  chunk's cells read the map's land. Three arrays a cell (60 MB on the nine, 800 MB on the island) and their loop at
  every bind.
- **The start's trees** (`StartTrees`): a chunk read when it first activates or a tree's drawing asks; the scene houses'
  walls counted only in the chunks round them on a generated map (the valley still looks at every cell).
- **A scoped look's cells to mark again**: a set, not a bool a cell. The ground's builder takes the land the world reads
  instead of reading `land.json` a second time (an int a cell).

Left as it was: `ChemGrid.MapWorld` paints every cell into the World and `KeepStart` keeps every cell (the `cells` step,
1.5 s on the nine); both write the core's World, which u2's sparse World (#204) is reworking, and `IChunkSource` is the
seam a chunk's rows can come through then. `BindStampedLand` and the core's `LandCragCells` / `LandLaidCells` still scan
the map when land is stamped (40-60 ms on the nine). `BakeLand` is the Editor's menu tool for the valley's land.json,
not a start-up cost. The near draw (25 chunks, 1.7-1.8 s) follows the reach.

Play mode. The bot's walk ran twice before and three times after.
