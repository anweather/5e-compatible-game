# Agent cost report, 2026-11-02

Covers commits on `dev` from 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-01. The last run was 2026-10-31. Full tables: `features-2026-11-02.md`, `issues-2026-11-02.md`.

- **Spend:** 320.8M ite in all: 144.5M building 107 features, 176.3M coordinating.
- **Overhead is 55% of spend** (59% last time; 52% on Nov 1 alone).
- **Cache reads are 66% of feature cost:** long sessions re-reading big contexts.
- **Median feature:** 1.03M ite, the same for the PC and the VMs.
- **Rediscovery rose to 13%** of discovery cost (6% last time), though see the caveats.

## Recommendations

1. **Restart the PM after each big batch, not each release.** Overhead is 55%, and it follows the clock more than the work.
2. **Script landing.** Fetch, diff stat, merge, gates and push need no reading unless a gate fails. Most of the PM's calls tonight were this.
3. **Clear host sessions per job, as tonight's clear did.** Host sessions cost 88.0M ite, and one reached about 958k tokens of context.
4. **Stop a stuck job early.** "The robe snaps back" cost 4.49M for 49 lines, and `castfeel-crossfade` 1.13M for 3: 9.5× and 6.5× over the fit.
5. **Batch small changes.** Cost goes as lines^0.36, so every task carries a fixed cost.
6. **Turn on cost trailers on the Mac,** and fill in `Discovery-Cost` with real numbers: both gaps below hide real spend.

## Method

`feature-costs.mjs` and `issue-costs.mjs` on `origin/dev`, with Coordinator and PM lines as overhead. Costs are in input-token equivalents (ite): input + 1.25 × cache write + 0.1 × cache read + 5 × output.
