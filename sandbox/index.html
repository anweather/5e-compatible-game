Spell Chemistry Sandbox

A top-down paper prototype of the game's spell interactions. Every reaction comes from the JSON rule tables the Unity build will load.

How to play
  1. You're the wizard: purple robe, pointed hat, soft ring. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD (click the board first so the keys reach it), or use the Move pad under the board.
  2. Pick a tool on the right: a spell, a critter, or a Build brush. 1–9 pick spells.
  3. Click a square to use it. Ray of Frost and Gust of Wind shoot out of the wizard toward where you click; Thunderwave goes off all around her; everything else lands on the square you click. The highlighted squares show what it will hit.
  4. Watch it play out and read What happened. Ice and grease are slippery, for you too.
  5. Play together: start a room and give friends the code. Experiments change the rules live for everyone; vote on what was fun.

What happened

    The reaction table

    Generated from rules/reactions.json. When an element reaches a square, the rules for that element run top to bottom; a rule marked "stops" ends the list for that square. Fire spreading, water flowing and gas drifting are the only parts written in code, and their numbers live in rules/sim.json.

    Spell names and descriptions draw on the System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dndbeyond.com/srd, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The physical readings of the spells (ice from Ray of Frost, flammable Grease) are this prototype's own.
